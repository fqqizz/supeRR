## Method

Final rank = average rank across five batting weight configurations x three cross-format blends (15 scenarios), and three bowling weight configurations x three blends (9 scenarios). Only players meeting minimum sample eligibility in both IPL and T20I entered the final pool. The 70% IPL / 30% T20I balanced model score is retained as a supporting reference; it is not the final ordering by itself.

## Caveats

This is a historical performance ranking, not a verified auction-availability list. Pressure is operationally defined as death phase OR five or more wickets lost before the delivery. Bowling boundary-event control is incomplete and is not official economy rate. Knockout samples were too sparse for reliable inclusion in the composite.
