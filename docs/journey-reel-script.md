**Style:** Direct-to-camera, calm and personal. Show the dashboard and report briefly between lines.

## Spoken script

"For SupeRR Selector Season 2, I wanted to answer one question properly: who performs best when a T20 innings gets difficult?

I started with the ball-by-ball data and built a pressure definition around two match situations: the death phase, and an innings where five wickets have already fallen.

Then I compared batters by role, measured death-over bowling, accounted for sample size, and tested the rankings under different scoring weights and IPL-T20I blends.

The result is not just two lists. It is a pressure-performance dashboard where the assumptions, player evidence and alternative scenarios can all be explored.

This is my approach to the challenge. Halla Bol!"
